LinkedIn outreach is live in SendEmAll.

You can add a LinkedIn seat next to your mailboxes, put LinkedIn steps in the same sequence as your
email steps, and work the replies from the same inbox. One thread per person, so a reply on either
channel stops the whole sequence instead of letting a scheduled follow-up land two days after they
already answered you.

Three decisions we made deliberately. The third one costs us money.

A connection request is a step you add, never a default. Accepting one permanently changes your own
network, and plenty of founders and execs guard theirs carefully. If you never add that step, we
never send one.

InMail cost is set by the recipient, not by you. Open Profile members can be messaged without
spending a credit, some members cannot be messaged at all, and LinkedIn returns the credit whenever
someone responds within 90 days — including a flat "not interested". The real cost of an InMail
campaign is the people who ignored you.

And the one that costs us: adding LinkedIn accounts does not multiply your reach. Each account
carries its own invitation cap, LinkedIn scopes that cap to the week, no subscription raises it, and
several accounts aimed at one audience is a correlation pattern. Throughput rises sub-linearly while
the risk rises with how alike the accounts look. We would rather say that now than sell you a fourth
seat you do not need.

LinkedIn seats are priced separately from the sequencer.
