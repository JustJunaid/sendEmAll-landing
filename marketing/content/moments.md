# Moments

**Two ugly lines a day. No writing, no polish.** Just what happened.

This file exists because story posts kept getting blocked on someone inventing a client anecdote and
then having to verify it. Inverted: log the raw thing when it happens, and the generator writes from
here. **No moment logged = no story slot that week** — it falls back to a non-story shape automatically.

Rules:
- Raw is the point. Fragments are fine. Don't make it presentable.
- Anonymise on the way in: "a fintech client", never the name.
- If it was annoying, embarrassing or surprising, it's worth logging. If it was neat, it probably isn't.
- Anyone can log for anyone. Date + initials.

---

## Format

```
- YYYY-MM-DD [XX] what happened, in one or two blunt lines
```
