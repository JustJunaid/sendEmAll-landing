# Brand Voice — SendEmAll (company X account: @SendEmAll)

## Identity
The product's own voice — not a person. Confident, useful, a little contrarian about the outbound
industry. Where Junaid is the founder's personal story and Sadiya is the practitioner, **the brand
account is the "category POV"**: it names the problems with the outbound status quo and shows the fix.

## Role in the mix
- **Junaid (@JustJunaidHere):** founder, first-person, vulnerable + data-backed.
- **Sadiya (LinkedIn + X):** practitioner/operator, execution stories, agency pipeline.
- **Brand (@SendEmAll):** category education, product ships, data drops, comparisons. Amplifies and
  is amplified BY the personal accounts (they quote-tweet each other — never in lockstep).

## Tone
- Crisp, declarative, useful. No hype, no emojis-as-personality.
- Teaches first. Product mentions are earned by the insight around them.
- "We tested…", "The math on outbound tooling:", "Here's what actually moves reply rate:".

## What the brand account posts
- **Ship notes** — a new feature framed as the pain it removes (from `ships.md`, `talkable: yes`).
- **Data drops** — real numbers from the platform (reply rates, cost breakdowns, deliverability).
- **Category takes** — "signal > spray", "the stack tax", "most leads aren't leads".
- **Comparisons** — honest, specific, our-transparency-vs-their-hidden-cost.

## Words to USE
signal, buying intent, the math, we tested, reply rate, deliverability, transparent, all-in-one, potential buyers

## Words to AVOID
AI-powered, revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, next-gen, synergy, leverage, unlock, empower, "leads" alone

## Guardrails (match the landing site's rules)
- Numbers everywhere ($600+, 3.4% vs 0.9%, 18 providers, $0.07/lead).
- "potential buyers" / "signal-qualified leads", never "leads" alone.
- Say "warmup" publicly (SEO); "Smart Ramp-Up" is internal only.
- Never expose mailbox-per-domain ratios.
- No selling in the post body — drive to profile/site, not hard links (also cheaper on X's per-post-with-link pricing).
